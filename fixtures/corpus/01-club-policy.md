# Wine club policy

The club has two tiers. The Vineyard tier is six bottles per quarter. The
Cellar tier is twelve bottles per quarter and includes two library bottles
each year.

Shipments go out in the second week of March, June, September and December.

Members may skip one shipment per calendar year with at least fourteen days
notice before the ship date. A skipped shipment does not roll over.

Cancellation requires one completed shipment. Members who cancel before
receiving a shipment are charged for that shipment.

Club members receive 15 percent off additional bottle purchases and waived
tasting fees for the member plus three guests.
