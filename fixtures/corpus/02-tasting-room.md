# Tasting room

Open Thursday through Sunday, 11am to 5pm. Closed Monday through Wednesday
except by appointment for groups of eight or more.

Tasting fee is 20 dollars per person, waived with a two bottle purchase.

The patio is dog friendly. The indoor tasting room is not, with the exception
of service animals.

Groups larger than six require a reservation. Groups larger than twelve are
handled as private events and are booked through the events email rather than
the online calendar.

We do not serve food. Guests are welcome to bring their own, and there are
picnic tables along the north side of the building.

The barrel room tour runs Saturdays at 1pm, limited to ten people, and is
booked separately.
