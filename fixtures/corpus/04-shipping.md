# Shipping

We ship to 34 states. We cannot ship to Utah, Mississippi, Alabama, Delaware,
Rhode Island, Kentucky, Arkansas or Hawaii.

An adult over 21 must sign for every delivery. Carriers will attempt delivery
three times before returning the package to us, and a returned package incurs
a 25 dollar restocking fee.

We hold shipments when the forecast at the destination exceeds 85 degrees or
falls below 28 degrees. Held shipments go out on the next suitable date and
members are notified by email.

Standard shipping is 5 to 7 business days. We do not offer overnight.

Orders placed after 2pm Friday are processed the following Monday.
