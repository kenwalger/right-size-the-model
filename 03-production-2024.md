# 2024 production notes

The 2024 harvest began 9 September, roughly ten days earlier than 2023.

Yields were down about 18 percent against a five year average, which we
attribute to the April frost event.

Pinot Noir was picked across three passes between 9 and 24 September. Brix at
pick ranged from 23.1 to 24.6.

The Pinot Gris came in on 12 September at 21.8 Brix and was whole cluster
pressed.

We did not bottle a rose in 2024. The fruit that would normally go to rose was
needed to make volume on the estate Pinot Noir.

Fermentation on the estate Pinot Noir ran 14 days, with a four day cold soak
beforehand.
